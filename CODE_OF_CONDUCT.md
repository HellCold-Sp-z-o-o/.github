# HellCold Collaboration Code

HellCold expects professional, respectful, factual, and security-conscious collaboration.

Contributors must:

- discuss people and technical decisions respectfully;
- give actionable review feedback supported by evidence;
- protect confidential, personal, customer, and security information;
- disclose conflicts of interest;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and disruptive conduct;
- follow repository ownership, review, and approval rules.

Report unacceptable conduct privately to HellCold Management. Reports will be handled confidentially and proportionately.
